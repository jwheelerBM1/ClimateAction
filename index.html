◈ The MAST Operational Paradigm

Mitigation, Adaptation, and Societal Transformation (MAST) expands traditional policy into a synchronized dual-timeline model to manage near-term and long-term climate shocks.

Section Purpose: Explore how the MAST framework dismantles the binary choice between mitigation and adaptation. By contrasting the 25-Year Sprint against the Century Marathon, users can inspect target levers, mechanisms, and structural goals across ecological, municipal, and societal dimensions.

Pillar 1 📉

Mitigation

Rapid abatement of Short-Lived Climate Pollutants (SLCPs) to cut warming rate by half, combined with long-term zero-carbon transition.

  • 40-45% Methane reduction by 2030
  • 85% Hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) phaseout
  • ~300B Tons atmospheric CO2 removal
Pillar 2 🌊

Adaptation

Exposure reduction and climate-proofing built and natural infrastructure through nature-based engineering models.

  • Sponge Planet nature-based solutions
  • Urban coastal & aquifer flood defense
  • Protection of water, food & health safety
Pillar 3 🤝

Societal Transformation

Systemic socio-economic restructuring focused on universal climate literacy, equity, human rights, and market reform.

  • Universal primary/secondary climate literacy
  • Green workforce transition funds
  • Protection for forced climate migrants

⏱️ Dual-Emergency Operational Framework

Near-Term Emergency The 25-Year Sprint

Aerosol Unmasking & SLCP Reduction

Phasing out fossil fuels rapidly removes cooling sulfate aerosols, uncovering unmasked latent warming. To avoid crossing irreversible tipping points (Greenland/WAIS ice collapse, AMOC shutdown), immediate action on methane, soot, and HFCs provides a crucial temporal bridge.

Core Horizon: 1 to 25 Years • Halves short-term warming rate.
Long-Term Emergency The Century Marathon

Economy-Wide Decarbonization & CDR

Achieving net-zero global CO2 emissions by mid-century while scaling Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) to extract 300 billion tons of legacy carbon from the atmosphere to stabilize Earth system thermal equilibrium over centuries.

Core Horizon: 25 to 100 Years • Full systemic decarbonization.

🌧️

Sponge Planet Model (SPM)

Kongjian Yu & Paul Kirshen

Replaces conventional "gray infrastructure" (concrete seawalls, damming) which accelerates flood velocity. SPM applies three core principles:

1. Retain at Source: Porous landscapes, urban wetlands & localized pond systems.
2. Slow Flow Down: Meandering blue-green waterways and low-elevation vegetation weirs.
3. Embrace Surplus: Floodable urban parks, terraced wetlands & elevated architecture.
🫁

Public Health & Clean Air Co-Benefits

Jos Lelieveld & Soumya Swaminathan

Empirical modeling confirms ambient PM2.5 and tropospheric ozone from fossil fuel combustion cause over 5 million premature deaths annually, concentrated heavily in East & South Asia.

Immediate Health Payoffs: Rapid reductions in ischemic heart disease, stroke, lung cancer, and persistent pediatric respiratory illnesses.
Climate Trauma: Clinical data (Jyoti Mishra) shows extreme heatwaves drive persistent PTSD, depression, and executive function impairments.
⚡

Food-Energy Aquavoltaics

Chien-Te Fan & Chia-Ling Lee

Resolving spatial conflicts between renewable solar expansion and agricultural/aquaculture land use using Taiwan aquavoltaic policy frameworks.

Utilizes the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to balance solar developer financial returns with local fishermen's livelihood stability and product marketing rights.

⚖️

Market Reform & Duty of Care

Daniel Esty, Wen-Chen Shih, Melissa Hoffer

Replacing traditional shareholder primacy with a legal stakeholder duty of care that eliminates uninternalized environmental externalities.

Leverages reformed WTO trade rules to discipline free-riders and deploys Green Banks to finance affordable green housing and municipal clean transit.
